1) Understand your need

Questions to clarify

  • What do you want to achieve — in one sentence?
  • Who benefits from it (you, your team, your customers)?
  • Is this a one-time tool or something you’ll use weekly?
  • Do you prefer a quick prototype or a solid long-term solution?
  • Free or paid? (see options below)

Clear answers help us choose the fastest, most affordable path for you.

2) Free vs paid — pick what suits you

Free (when it fits)

  • Very small tasks or guidance (“how to…”)
  • Short reviews / quick fixes if trivial
  • Pointing you to templates or ready snippets

We volunteer when it’s quick and clearly helpful.

Paid (recommended for results)

  • Real deliverables (scripts, bots, dashboards)
  • Anti-bot/proxy rotation, reliability, monitoring
  • Milestones, documentation, optional maintenance

You get focused time, higher quality and ongoing support.
