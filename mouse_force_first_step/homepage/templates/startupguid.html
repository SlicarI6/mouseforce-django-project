MouseForce How to Ask MouseForce

Ask better. Get better answers.

MouseForce can help research offers, places, experiences, tools and practical options. The more useful detail you give us, the easier it is to narrow things down.

Contact MouseForce

1. Tell us what you need

Explain the goal in one clear sentence.

  • Find me a busy London nightclub for Saturday.
  • Find a hotel in Dubai near a specific area.
  • Find a current UK discount for cinema tickets.
  • Help me understand whether a repetitive browser task can be automated.

2. Add the details that matter

  • Location
  • Date or time
  • Budget
  • Age range if relevant
  • Solo or group
  • Busy or quiet
  • Any important restrictions or preferences

You do not need to write perfectly. Clear details are more useful than long messages.

3

We narrow down the options

MouseForce can compare suitable options, check useful conditions and help reduce unnecessary searching.

4

You decide

We help organise the information and present useful options. The final choice is always yours.

Examples of useful requests

Nightlife

Find me a busy London club for Saturday, age 25–35, where solo entry is allowed.

Dating & Social

Find a social event in London where it is normal to arrive alone.

Travel

Find a hotel in Dubai near this area for under £120 per night.

Discounts

Find a current cinema discount available in the UK.

Automation

I repeat the same browser task every day. Can this be made easier?

The more specific you are, the better we can help.

A few useful details can save a lot of unnecessary searching.
