Playbooks

SEO basics (2-hour setup)

  1. Connect domain to Search Console & submit sitemap.
  2. Fix obvious issues: titles, H1, internal links.
  3. Create 3 helpful pages answering real questions.
  4. Get 3 relevant backlinks (partners, communities).

Measure weekly impressions & clicks in GSC.

Content marketing

  1. Pick 3 themes customers care about.
  2. Publish 1 useful article per week (problem → solution).
  3. Repurpose each piece into 3 short posts.
  4. Send a monthly email with highlights.

Email automation

  1. Welcome + value email (day 0).
  2. Two educational emails (day 2 & 5).
  3. Soft pitch with case/example (day 8).

Use Mailchimp/Brevo; keep opt-in clean.

Landing pages & CRO

  1. Single CTA above the fold.
  2. 1 problem → 1 promise → 1 proof → 1 action.
  3. A/B test headline; track with UTM.
