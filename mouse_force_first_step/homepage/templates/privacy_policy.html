Who we are

Mouseforce builds scripts, automations, scraping pipelines, job-finding bots, and testing tools. We also deliver small/medium turnkey projects. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal data when you visit our websites, contact us, or use our services.In the near future, we will focus on enhancing our system’s Help Center to enable rapid hiring of skilled workers.

Data we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact form data: name, business email, company, locations, and your message.
  • Account/project info: requirements, technical preferences, credentials you explicitly provide (e.g., API keys) for integration. We strongly recommend using least-privilege keys.
  • Support messages: emails, tickets, or chat communications.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & device data: IP address, timestamps, pages, user-agent. We keep this minimal for debugging, security and service quality.
  • Cookies/local storage: your consent choice and essential settings (see Cookies).
Note on scraping: We only scrape data you instruct us to process and/or data that is public or authorized by terms. We follow rate limits, robots guidance where applicable, and anti-abuse rules agreed in the contract.

How we use personal data

  • Provide and improve services — development, deployment, maintenance.
  • Operate automations — e.g., job notifications via email/Discord/Telegram.
  • Security & fraud prevention — detect abuse and protect infrastructure.
  • Analytics (lightweight) — diagnose performance and UX issues.
  • Communications — replies to inquiries, service notices, invoices.
  • Compliance — legal obligations and dispute handling.
  • Portfolio demos (anonymized) — only with your explicit permission.

Legal bases (EU/UK GDPR)

  • Contract — to deliver the services you request.
  • Legitimate interests — security, quality, basic analytics.
  • Consent — cookies or optional notifications where required.
  • Legal obligation — invoices, records, regulatory requests.

Data retention

We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above: project files and logs are kept for the service lifecycle, then archived or deleted. You can request deletion where applicable (see Your rights).

Sharing & vendors

  • Service providers — hosting, CI/CD, email, storage, payment processors.
  • Sub-contractors — when needed for delivery, under NDA and strict DPAs.
  • Legal authorities — only if we must comply with the law.
We never sell personal data. Access is limited to the minimum required. Credentials (e.g., API keys) are stored securely with encryption at rest or kept in your own vault when possible.

International transfers

Depending on the stack you choose, data may be processed in the UK, EU, or other regions. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g., SCCs) with our vendors.

Security

  • Principle of least privilege and environment separation.
  • Encrypted storage for secrets; rotate keys on project completion when feasible.
  • Structured logs and monitoring to detect anomalies.
  • Regular dependency updates and patching.

Your rights

Subject to your jurisdiction (e.g., EU/UK/US state laws):

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (a copy in a common format).
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on it.
  • Complain to your supervisory authority.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies (e.g., remembering your consent) and limited analytics. You can update your preference from the banner or your browser settings. If you previously consented and want to reset, clear site data in your browser and reload the page to see the banner again.

Product-specific notes

Game scripts

We process in-game identifiers only when needed for the requested features (economies, progress, admin tools). We don’t track players beyond what you instruct.

Scraping & automations

We scrape only public/authorized sources per your instructions, respect rate limits, and include backoff/headers/rotations to avoid service abuse.

Job-finding bots

We parse job posts, score them against rules you define, and notify on matches. You can disable or delete your rules at any time.

Test scripting

We run automated tests against environments you control. Test logs may include metadata required to investigate failures.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy to reflect our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be highlighted here with a new “Last updated” date.
