How Points Work

MouseForce Points are simple: collect them, save them, and spend them on rewards and offers you choose. From time to time, special offers may also become available, giving you more ways to use your points.

The longer you stay with MouseForce and the more active you are, the more opportunities you may unlock. We value loyal and active customers and aim to reward genuine engagement with more benefits over time.

Daily Points

Claim 10 points every day and keep track of your total balance. When you use your points, you’ll always be able to see how many you have left.

7 Day Streak

A 7-day streak always leads to something good. Stay consistent, claim your points every day, and earn an extra 35 points as a reward for your commitment.

14 Day Streak

Complete a full 14-day streak and earn up to 225 points in total.

14 daily claims × 10 points = 140 points
7 Day Streak bonus = +35 points
14 Day Streak bonus = +50 points
Total = 225 points

What happens if you miss a day?

Nothing bad happens if you miss a day — your total points stay safe. Your streak will reset, and any unclaimed streak bonus may be lost, but you can start building a new streak with your next daily claim.
